### 2. A month's first recall stays on the page twice once the month gets a second: CPSC and EPA group at ingest, under a different key · `P2`

**Evidence**

*The two keys.*

*The replay* (`audit100/probes/cpsc_orphans.ts`): the service read every 7 days (`READ_EVERY_DAYS`, `lib/ingest/cpscWalk.ts:13`) from 2025-01-01 to 2026-11-05, and the company's rows stored after each read.

### 5. The archive's 2005–2008 pages write the possessive as `&#8216;`, and the Justice rule misses five merger cases on four companies · `P3`

**How sure:** high. Counterfactual replay on the committed pages.

**Evidence**

*The pages.*
- 83 archive titles hold a letter, "‘", then "s" (`audit100/probes/doj_quotes.ts`).
- The bytes on the 2005-10 page are `VERIZON&#8216;S` (`doj_quote_bytes.ts`). `plainText` reads them as "‘" (U+2018).

*The rule.* Only `['’]` counts as an apostrophe: `DEAL` (`lib/doj.ts:77`), `FOLLOWS` (`:74`) and `escapeName` (`:113-121`).

*What reading "‘s" as "’s" adds* (`doj_archive_replay_q.ts`; 127 → 132 company-days):
- VZ 2005-10-27, "VERIZON‘S ACQUISITION OF MCI";
- WHR 2006-03-29, Maytag;
- T 2006-10-11, BellSouth, and 2007-10-30, Dobson;
- CX 2007-04-04, Rinker.

*They would reach pages.* First prices: VZ and T 1983-11-21, WHR 1973-02-21, CX 1999-09-15.

*How it was recorded.* Batch 94's claim, "reads &#146; … and the other Windows-1252 codes" (`MASTER-CHECKLIST.md:1271`), is true. But &#8216; is not one of them.

**Failure scenario:** Whirlpool's page lacks the Justice Department's closing of its Maytag investigation. Verizon's lacks the divestitures in its MCI acquisition.

**Smallest fix:** read a "‘" between a letter and "s" as "’" in `plainText`. Or add "‘" to the three apostrophe classes.

**Check:** pin WHR 2006-03-29 in `check:doj`. The archive replay's 127/106 counts move.

*Importance:* low-medium: missing events · *Efficiency:* one character class · *Practicality:* trivial.
