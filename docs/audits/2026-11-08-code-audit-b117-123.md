## Findings

### 1. Southern Natural Gas stays Kinder Morgan's after Southern Company Gas bought into it in 2016: two EPA rows on KMI's page, and the release map has no end day either · `P3`

**How sure:** high that an interest changed hands on 2016-08-31/09-01 and that neither map ends there. Medium that it was the half share the joint-venture rule refuses: the filings' text is on www.sec.gov, which this run could not read.

**Evidence**
- **The unit.** `unit("KMI", "El Paso's companies", /^SOUTHERN NATURAL GAS\b/i, "southernnaturalgas", { from: "2012-05-24" })` (`lib/epaCases.ts:951`) has no `to`.
- **The release map** mirrors it: `op("Southern Natural Gas", "2012-05-24")` (`lib/releaseNames.ts:766`). `releaseNamesOn(1506307, "2021-01-01")` returns it (`out/kmi_sng.txt`).
- **The filings** (data.sec.gov lists):
  - Southern Company Gas (CIK 1004155): an 8-K filed 2016-09-01, report 2016-08-31, items 1.01, 2.01 and 9.01, accession 0001004155-16-000204. Its primary document is `sogas8-kassignofsonat.htm` (`b114/dl/sub-agl/body`); Sonat was the old name of SNG's parent.
  - Kinder Morgan's own list holds 8-Ks with items 7.01 and 9.01 reporting 2016-07-10 (filed 2016-07-11) and 2016-09-01 (`net/kmi-sub/body`).
- **The rows.** Replayed on EPA's whole file, from KMI's first price, 2011-02-11 (`out/kmi_sng.txt`, `out/cases_utc_sng.txt`):
  - 2020-07-09, "EPA final order (Clean Air Act)", 06-2020-3372: an order on consent to "Southern Natural Gas Co, LLC" for its Franklinton Compressor Station;
  - 2021-08-18, "EPA consent decree with 33 parties (Superfund law (CERCLA))", 02-2010-0008, "against Southern Natural Gas Company and 32 other defendants".
- **The project's rule.** "a joint venture no one's" (`CLAUDE.md:94`); "Half of a business is a joint venture, never the company's" (`lib/recalls.ts:65-66`). The `UNITS` header gives El Paso's pipelines "from 2012-05-24" and no end (`lib/epaCases.ts:547-549`), and so does the ledger (`MASTER-CHECKLIST.md:1193-1194`).

**Failure scenario:** KMI's page shows a joint venture's 2020 Clean Air Act order and 2021 Superfund decree as Kinder Morgan's own. The release rules would also give KMI any Justice, SEC or CFTC release that names SNG after 2016.

**What a fix would need:** read the share and the closing day from the 8-K's text, which needs `NEWS_CHARTS_CONTACT` for www.sec.gov (OWNER-ACTIONS §2). Then give the unit and the op name a `to` of the day before the sale. A check would hold that 06-2020-3372 is on no page and that SNG's 2013 and earlier rows stay on KMI.

*Importance:* low-medium: two rows, no dollar figure · *Efficiency:* two `to` fields once the day is read · *Practicality:* easy, but it waits on the contact.

**How sure:** high.

**Evidence**
