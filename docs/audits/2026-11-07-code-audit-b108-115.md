  - "AT&T INC" in NL Industries' decree of 1999-01-05;

**Smallest fix:** a `NOT_THE_COMPANY` entry for 732717, `/^AT\s*&\s*T\b/i` with `before: "2005-11-18"`, as for WM. A check could also list every index company whose present EDGAR name began after its first price and require a reviewed entry for each. That would end fixing the class one page at a time.

### 5. EPA's summary shows placeholders the filter misses, and lost characters are misread as quotation marks · `P3`

**Evidence**
- **Placeholders shown as "EPA's summary".** `SUMMARY_SAYS_NOTHING` (`lib/epaCases.ts:125-133`) has "case summary requested" but not "submitted", nor the others below. On the replay's page rows:
  - "NO CASE SUMMARY SUBMITTED", 25 characters, exactly the floor: 4 rows (ECL, D, BWXT, ROOT);

- **Lost characters.** `summaryOf` reads U+FFFD as an apostrophe only between a letter and an "s" (`:155`), and otherwise as a quotation mark (`:157`). 39 page cases hold one. The misreads:
  - **a plural possessive becomes a quote.** LNT's $1 billion 2013 decree reads 'modifications at Defendants" Nelson Dewey, Columbia and Edgewater'. Also "United States" claims" (02-2017-0013 on OXY, PPG, SHW, WS) and "customers" facilities" (04-2020-2500 on BA, LMT);

**Smallest fix:**
- add "submitted", "will provide", "see … summary" and a bare step-and-date sentence to the patterns;
- read U+FFFD after "s" and before a space as an apostrophe;
- read a lone U+FFFD between spaces as a dash, and any run of 1–4 before a digit as section signs.

*Importance:* low: wrong punctuation and empty text, no wrong figure · *Efficiency:* a few patterns · *Practicality:* easy.
