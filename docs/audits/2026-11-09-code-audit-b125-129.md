## Findings

### 1. Railroad accident rows give the wrong death and injury counts: the reader takes FRA's per-record Form 54 counts, which the dataset says are not the accident's · `P1`

**How sure:** high. The dataset's own descriptions and its per-accident fields were read this run.

**Evidence**
- **What the dataset says** (`net/fra-meta/body`, its column descriptions, saved in `out/fra_meta_counts.txt`):
  - `totalkilledform54`: "Total killed as reported on Form 54. Do not use this field to sum fatalities." (`totalinjuredform54` says the same of injuries.)
  - `totalpersonskilled`: "Total number of people killed in the accident as reported on Form 55a. This field contains the total fatalities for all reports filled for the single accident."
  - `reportingrailroadfatalitiesfor55a`: "The JOINTCD=1 record contains the total number and other reports contain zero. This is the official fatality count per accident, not per report."
- **What the code reads:** only the Form 54 fields. They are in `FIELDS` (`lib/railAccidents.ts:214-217`) and in the query's floor, `totalkilledform54 > 0` (`:226`). A report's records are merged by their larger count (`larger(...)`, `:334-335`; "with the largest count each record gives", `:16-17`). The floor (`:308-311`) and the title (`:352-356`, `:366`) both read the merged count.
- **What the owner approved:** "The death count is everyone the accident killed, crew and public." (`docs/proposals/2026-11-09-proposals.md:274`).
- **Goodwell, Oklahoma** (Union Pacific 0612KC011, 2012-06-24). Every field of its two records is in `net/fra-goodwell/body`:
  - the `jointcd` 1 record gives Form 54 counts of 2 killed and 0 injured, and the `jointcd` 3 record 1 and 1;
  - both give `totalpersonskilled` 3 and `totalpersonsinjured` 1;
  - the `jointcd` 1 record's `reportingrailroadfatalitiesfor55a` is 3.

  The two records are the two trains' parts of one count, so the larger of them is not the accident's. The page row reads "Union Pacific: head-on collision at Goodwell, Oklahoma — 2 killed, 1 injured", and check:rail-accidents pins that as right (`scripts/check-rail-accidents.ts:109-112`).
- **Across the six pages** (`out/fra_counts.txt`). The committed fixture was run through `accidentEvents` with the check's first prices and compared with the per-accident totals in `net/fra-totals`:
  - **21 of the 476 rows show fewer deaths than the accident's count.** 7 are reports of several records merged by the larger count. 14 are single reports whose own Form 54 count leaves out the accident's dead, and 13 of those show no death at all. Among them:
    - CSX, 1993-09-22: "CSX Transportation: derailment at Akka, Alabama — $11,369,400 of damage". Per accident: 47 killed, 103 injured.
    - Union Pacific, 2008-09-12: "Union Pacific: head-on collision at Chatsworth, California — 3 injured". Per accident: 25 killed, 61 injured.
    - Union Pacific, 2005-01-26, Glendale: "— $13,020,300 of damage". Per accident: 11 killed.
    - CN, 1999-03-15, Bourbonnais: "— 1 injured". Per accident: 11 killed, 50 injured.
    - CSX, 1996-02-16, Georgetown, Maryland: "— $6,642,000 of damage". Per accident: 11 killed.
    - Berkshire Hathaway, 2021-09-25, Joplin, Montana: "— $22,019,251 of damage". Per accident: 3 killed, 83 injured.
  - **44 rows show fewer injuries** than the accident's count.
  - **14 fatal accidents are on no page.** Each is in a mapped railroad's days and after its company's first price, and killed someone by the per-accident count. But every Form 54 record says 0, and none reached the damage or evacuation floor. The largest is CSX's of 1991-07-31 (CSX079105051199107), 8 killed.
  - **2 rows show a death the per-accident count does not have:** CN's of 2022-08-25 (IC1112131202208) and CSX's of 2016-09-06 (CSX000162754201609). FRA's two counts disagree on these.
- **Why the check passed:** the fixture holds neither per-accident field (`FIELDS`), so check:rail-accidents could only confirm the reader's own reading.


**What a fix would need:**
- Read `totalpersonskilled` and `totalpersonsinjured`, or the `jointcd` 1 record's `reportingrailroadfatalitiesfor55a` and `…injuriesfor55a`, for both the floor and the title. Ask the query by `totalpersonskilled > 0` as well.
- Capture a new fixture from the live service with those fields. Re-pin Goodwell at 3 killed and Big Bayou Canot at 47, and pin one of the 14 missing accidents.
- The owner should say what the two rows where Form 54 shows a death and Form 55a none should show.

*Importance:* high: the most visible number on a railroad page, wrong most on the worst accidents · *Efficiency:* two fields in one reader and a new fixture · *Practicality:* easy; one request for the fixture.

**How sure:** high that these releases land on no page. Whether to add the names is the batch's own aim, not a new call.

**Evidence**

- **Batch 128's own saved answers** to `title=DePuy`, `title=Synthes` and `title=Teva Pharmaceuticals` (`b128/dl/api-depuy`, `api-synthes`, `api-teva_pharmaceuticals`) were replayed for every index company with the release map, from first prices (`out/health_api.txt`). No page takes these:
