### 2. RGC Resources' page shows the new parent's shell reports beside Roanoke Gas's for the same quarters · `P3`

**How sure:** high. Both records' 10-Qs were read.

**Evidence**

**What users see:** two quarterly reports for each of the quarters to December 1998 and March 1999, one of them a shell's with no business in it.

**What a fix would need:** leave the successor's own filings before the handover off where they are the shell's: a `since`-like day for the successor, or the two accessions listed. Pin it in check:predecessors.
