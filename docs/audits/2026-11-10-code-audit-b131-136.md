## Findings

### 1. Kroger Specialty Pharmacy's 2026 EPA order is on Kroger's page, two years after Kroger sold the pharmacy · `P3`

**How sure:** medium-high. The sale's completion is bounded by EDGAR's full-text search metadata. The filings' text could not be read here: www.sec.gov needs a contact.

**Evidence**
- **The unit has no end day.** `lib/epaCases.ts:1173`:
  ```
  unit("KR", "Kroger's partnerships, plants and pharmacies", /^(?:THE\s+)?KROGER\s+(?:LIMITED PARTNERSHIP|TEXAS|MEMPHIS|SPECIALTY PHARMACY|CO(?:MPANY)?\.?\s*(?:\(|D\/B\/A|BAKERY))/i, "kroger"),
  ```
  The ledger counts the pharmacy as Kroger's on every day ("Kroger's plants and pharmacy", `docs/MASTER-CHECKLIST.md:1071`).
- **What it takes on EPA's whole file** (`probes/epa_hits.ts`, `out/epa_edges.txt`). Its last defendant is `2026-07-28 02-2026-7204 own Kroger Specialty Pharmacy 5-60 (Axium Healthcare)`.
- **On Kroger's page** from its first price (`out/epa_b135_rows.json`): `{'id': 'epa-56873-2026-07-28', 'date': '2026-07-28', 'title': 'EPA final order: $1,250 civil penalty (hazardous waste law (RCRA))', 'externalId': '02-2026-7204'}`.
- **The sale is done by 2024-11-09.** The phrase "gain on sale of Kroger Specialty Pharmacy" first appears in Kroger's results release of 2024-12-05 (8-K, item 2.02) and its 10-Q for the quarter ending 2024-11-09 (`net/efts-ksp-gain/body`, 9 hits, none earlier). The name also appears in the 10-K of 2024-04-02, filed with its subsidiaries exhibit (`net/efts-ksp/body`). A gain on a sale is booked when the sale closes.
- **No check holds the pharmacy's days.** check-epa-cases' Kroger check (`scripts/check-epa-cases.ts:736`) covers only the partnerships.

**What users see:** a $1,250 hazardous-waste order on Kroger's timeline, dated 2026-07-28, for a pharmacy another company owned by then.

**What a fix would need:**
- Give "SPECIALTY PHARMACY" a unit of its own with a `to` day, read from Kroger's filing list (the 8-K or 10-Q reporting the closing), as batch 120's rule does.
- Pin 02-2026-7204 as on no page.
- Decide whether the buyer's page should carry it. The buyer is CarelonRx, Elevance Health's unit; the batch 120 rule ("a unit sold to another listed company on that company's page from the day") would put it there.

*Importance:* low-medium: one wrong-company enforcement row · *Efficiency:* one day and one check · *Practicality:* easy; one data.sec.gov list.

### 2. The CPSC injury statement is cut mid-word on 14 rows, with no mark, and the check counts those rows as carrying it · `P3`

**How sure:** high.

**Evidence**
- **Where the statement sits.** `cpscEvents` puts it last, after the title, units and hazard (`lib/cpscRecalls.ts:522`). The row is then cut at 600 characters with no ellipsis (`:535`, `:548`).
- **What happens on the real recalls.** `probes/cpsc_cut.ts` runs every listed company against the committed recalls with the check's own population (`out/cpsc_cut.txt`). Of the 952 maker, importer or distributor rows whose first recall has a statement:
  - 938 show it whole;
  - **14 are cut mid-statement**;
  - none lose it entirely.

  Across every recall on those rows, 959 of 986 statements are shown whole. Some cuts drop what the Commission said about the outcome (`probes/cpsc_lost.py`, `out/cpsc_lost.txt`):
  - **Polaris, 2021-03-25 (21732):** "…resulting in a damaged fuel line, includ". Lost: "…ing two reports of fire, neither of which involved property damage other than to the vehicle. No injuries have…".
  - **Williams-Sonoma, 2009-12-15 (10071):** "…The child has r". Lost: "…ecovered from the thin laceration around his neck. Roller Shades: There have been no reports of injuries."
  - **IBM, 2006-09-28 (06270):** "…no injuries were report". **Mattel, 2006-11-21 (07039):** "…An 8-year-old child was also h".
- **What the check counts.** It counts a row as carrying a statement when the description holds the prefix "Injuries, the Commission's record: " (`scripts/check-cpsc.ts:299-303`). The ledger, the README (`:861`) and the check all say "952 of the 1,121 rows … carry one". As whole statements, 938 do.
- **The same batch marks its cuts elsewhere.** MSHA's account (`lib/mineAccidents.ts:414`) and FRA's narrative (`lib/railAccidents.ts`, `describe`) cut at a word and close with "…".

**What users see:** a regulator's statement presented "as the Commission wrote it", stopping mid-word. In Polaris's case the cut drops the sentence saying no one was hurt.

**What a fix would need:**
- Cut the statement at a word with "…", or give the statement the room first and cut the hazard instead.
- Make the check compare against the recall's whole statement.

*Importance:* low: 14 rows, no number wrong · *Efficiency:* one function · *Practicality:* easy.
